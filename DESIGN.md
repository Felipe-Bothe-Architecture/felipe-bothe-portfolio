# Design System Document: Atelier Minimal

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star: "The Architectural Monolith"

This design system is built for high-end professionals where the work must speak as loudly as the silence. The aesthetic is defined by "The Architectural Monolith"—a philosophy of structural honesty, monumental proportions, and a tactile relationship between light and material. Every element should feel intentional, permanent, and prestigious.

## 2. Visual Principles

*   **Negative Space as Luxury:** White space is not "empty"—it is a premium design element. Use generous margins and padding to allow content to "breathe" and to elevate the importance of every image and line of text.
*   **Structural Honesty:** Layouts should follow a clear, often asymmetrical grid that reflects architectural blueprints. Use thin, precise lines for separation rather than heavy borders or shadows.
*   **Materiality & Light:** Imagery should focus on the interplay of light and shadow, highlighting natural textures (stone, wood, concrete).
*   **Prestige through Restraint:** Avoid flashy animations or vibrant colors. Impact is achieved through scale, high-quality typography, and high-resolution imagery.

## 3. Color Palette (The Earth & Stone Palette)

*   **Primary Background (Surface):** `#FBF9F4` (Alabaster/Bone) - A warm, off-white that feels more organic and luxurious than pure white.
*   **Secondary Surface:** `#F5F4ED` - For subtle tonal shifts in sections or footers.
*   **Primary Text/Brand:** `#4A453E` (Charcoal Umber) - A deep, warm grey for primary headings and brand elements.
*   **Secondary/Body Text:** `#635E56` - A slightly softer grey for long-form reading and metadata.
*   **Accent/Call to Action:** `#635E56` - Used with high contrast or underline for interactivity. No vibrant accent colors.
*   **Dark Mode Surface (Optional):** `#1C1917` (Stone-950) - For high-contrast "night" views.

## 4. Typography (The Noto & Manrope Pairing)

*   **Primary Serif (Noto Serif):** Used for Headlines and Brand Identity.
    *   **Style:** Low-contrast, sturdy serifs.
    *   **Usage:** Large scale (72px+ for heroes), tracking should be tight or standard.
*   **Secondary Sans (Manrope):** Used for UI elements, labels, and secondary navigation.
    *   **Style:** Clean, geometric sans-serif.
    *   **Usage:** Uppercase with wide tracking (0.1rem - 0.2rem) for a modern, architectural feel.

## 5. UI Components & Patterns

### Top Navigation Bar
*   **Layout:** Fixed, transparent with a subtle backdrop-blur (80% surface color).
*   **Links:** Uppercase Manrope, wide tracking. Active states indicated by a thin 1px border-bottom or slight opacity change.
*   **Logo:** Noto Serif, uppercase, high tracking.

### Image Treatment
*   **Scale:** Images should be full-bleed or large-scale within the grid.
*   **Composition:** Minimalist, high-quality architectural or product photography.

### Technical Synthesis (Data Visualization)
*   **Grid:** Use a thin 1px light-grey grid background for technical sections.
*   **Labels:** Numbered lists (01, 02, 03) in Manrope Bold for a schematic look.

### Buttons / CTAs
*   **Primary:** Solid `#635E56` with white text, or ghost buttons with a 1px border. No rounded corners (unless specified as 'Roundness Unspecified'—default to sharp 0px or very subtle 2px).
*   **Interactions:** Subtle fade or underline on hover.

## 6. Layout Strategy

*   **Desktop:** Asymmetrical grid with mixed column widths. Use "Next Project" footers that fill the entire screen width to create a continuous flow.
*   **Mobile:** Stacked vertical layout, maintaining generous vertical spacing. Navigation collapses into a minimal "Menu" text or icon.

## 7. Tone of Voice

Direct, professional, and understated. Use descriptive but concise language. Avoid marketing jargon; focus on craft and philosophy.
